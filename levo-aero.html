Challenge

A premium service needs a clear price story.

Enterprise customers evaluating a private aviation platform need to understand what each tier includes and how to buy it. Those pages needed to exist as distinct, shareable URLs that sales conversations could point to.

Strategy

One page per tier, one clear path.

  • Give each Enterprise tier its own landing page and unique URL for customer-facing use.
  • Reuse proven page structures so the experience stays consistent with the rest of the site.
  • Support awareness with clean, on-brand ad creative sized for LinkedIn and Instagram.
My Role

Builder and designer.

  • Built four Enterprise pricing landing pages in the development CMS, starting from existing pages and adapting them to tiered pricing.
  • Designed paid-social ad creative in Canva for LinkedIn and Instagram placements.
Execution

Pages first, then promotion.

  • Duplicated and restructured existing pages so all four tiers share a consistent layout.
  • Set up unique URLs per tier for direct customer use.
  • Produced ad layouts in feed, story and LinkedIn sizes.
Results

Delivered — no metrics claimed.

The four Enterprise pricing pages were delivered with unique, customer-facing URLs. Levo is not part of the Sprout Social export and I don't have campaign or conversion data for this work, so I'm not reporting any performance figures.

Key Takeaways

What I'd carry forward.

  1. Pricing pages are sales tools — one URL per tier makes them easy to share in a deal.
  2. Next step: add analytics events to each tier page so marketing can see which tier gets the most interest.
