Challenge

A performance report needs context.

These brands had different paid-media questions. Axe & Kindle needed a review of account measurement and campaign efficiency. Every Window needed to connect campaign reports with its store and configurator funnel. Levo needed an acquisition plan and a clear set of launch prerequisites.

The shared challenge: decide what to fix or test before committing more media spend.

Strategy

Measurement, intent, then investment.

  • Check the signal: review conversion definitions and value reporting before drawing conclusions about efficiency.
  • Separate intent: distinguish branded, product, research and business-buyer searches.
  • Follow the click: connect the ad message to a relevant product, configurator, quote or demo path.
  • Make recommendations testable: specify the next action, required access and KPI for each priority.
My Role

Account review, reporting analysis, launch planning.

  • Axe & Kindle: conducted a view-only Google Ads audit covering tracking, campaign allocation, search terms, copy, landing pages and a proposed B2B lead campaign.
  • Every Window: analyzed the GA4/Data Studio sales report and monthly performance sheet. I did not have direct Google Ads account access; search terms, bidding settings and conversion configuration remained items to verify.
  • Levo Aero: reviewed the website and product materials, defined audience-specific campaign ideas and drafted a phased acquisition plan with measurement dependencies.
Execution

Three brands, different next steps.

  • Axe & Kindle: prioritized conversion-value validation and purchase/lead tracking, then recommended reviewing campaign allocation, search intent and product-page clarity.
  • Every Window: flagged inconsistent conversion counts across reporting systems, recommended investigating branded-search efficiency and identified quote tracking and cart recovery as opportunities.
  • Levo Aero: proposed starting with operator and broker intent on Google Search, supported by LinkedIn and remarketing once tracking and conversion paths were ready. Traveler acquisition was a later, limited test.
Results

Delivered recommendations. Impact to be measured.

The deliverables were an account audit, a reporting-based campaign analysis and a paid-acquisition growth plan. Each identified priorities, dependencies and measures for evaluating the next step.

Status at the time of these documents: the Axe & Kindle review was view-only, Every Window recommendations required account access and approval, and Levo was a proposed launch plan. These documents do not establish implementation or post-change results. No revenue lift, ROAS improvement, cost reduction or lead growth is claimed.

Sources: my Axe & Kindle Google Ads Audit (Oct 26, 2026; account period Sep 26–Oct 25); Every Window Google Ads Analysis (Oct 28, 2026; campaign period Sep 28–Oct 27, with separate funnel and monthly comparison periods); Levo Paid Ads Growth Plan (website review dated Oct 28, 2026). Historical findings describe those reviews, not the current state of the accounts or websites.

Key Takeaways

What I would validate next.

  1. Confirm that purchase, quote and lead events mean the same thing across the reports used to make decisions.
  2. Review search terms and landing-page intent before treating a campaign label as evidence of buyer quality.
  3. Use qualified leads and completed purchases to evaluate a test; do not substitute clicks for business outcomes.
  4. Record a baseline, implement approved changes and compare equivalent periods before attributing an improvement.

Analysis & proposed next steps

Axe & Kindle · Validate the signal

Google Ads account audit · View-only · Oct 26, 2026

Finding

Conversion-value adjustments and purchase-tracking warnings made reported efficiency a priority to validate. Business inquiries also needed a defined lead conversion.

Recommended action

Confirm whether value adjustments are intentional, review purchase event data and add contact-form lead measurement before testing a business-buyer campaign.

How to measure

Validated purchase values and event counts; successful test lead submissions; purchase efficiency and cost per qualified inquiry after approved changes.

Analysis & proposed next steps

Every Window · Connect intent to the funnel

GA4/Data Studio report + monthly comparison sheet · Oct 28, 2026

Finding

Campaign efficiency varied, while Google Ads, GA4 and store reports showed different conversion counts. Quote and configurator activity needed clearer measurement.

Recommended action

Request account access to inspect branded search, search terms and conversion settings; review lower-efficiency campaigns; connect quote, call and cart-recovery paths to reporting.

How to measure

Reconciled conversion definitions; branded CPC and conversion efficiency; qualified quotes and calls; cart-to-order rate. Account-level recommendations remain subject to verification.

Analysis & proposed next steps

Levo Aero · Build a measurable launch

Paid Ads Growth Plan · Proposed strategy · Oct 28, 2026

Finding

Operators, brokers and travelers needed different messages and conversion paths. The plan identified measurement and website prerequisites before a paid launch.

Recommended action

Validate tracking and sign-up/demo paths, research keyword demand, then test operator and broker Search campaigns with LinkedIn support. Introduce remarketing when its prerequisites are met.

How to measure

Qualified operator demo requests, broker sign-ups, landing-page conversion rate and cost per qualified lead. Establish baselines from actual campaign data before setting performance targets.
