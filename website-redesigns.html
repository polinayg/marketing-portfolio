Challenge

Useful content needs a clear hierarchy.

Both homepages had to introduce the brand, explain the offer and help visitors choose their next step. Every Window combines window selection, installation information and buyer education. Axe & Kindle combines firewood shopping, live-fire cooking, delivery information and trust signals.

The earlier captures already contain substantial content. My redesign work focused on how that content is presented and organized.

Strategy

Keep the brand. Clarify the journey.

  • Lead with the offer: use the opening section to establish the brand and a visible action.
  • Group related decisions: bring product or style selection, installation guidance and supporting information into recognizable sections.
  • Make trust easier to find: give customer reviews and supporting content a defined place in the page.
  • Carry the visual system through: apply consistent brand colors, section treatments and footer presentation.
My Role

The redesign, shown in context.

I built nxtdib.com from scratch as part of my multi-brand marketing work. I also redesigned the Every Window and Axe & Kindle homepages; the supplied screenshots document changes to layout, content presentation, imagery and calls to action. My broader website work also includes redesigning levo.aero.

Execution

Two brands, two visual systems.

Every Window: reorganized the installation introduction, changed the installation video from a full-width block to a text-and-video section, brought customer reviews higher into the page, and turned the style section into a visual selection grid. The redesigned footer uses a stronger deep-teal treatment.

Axe & Kindle: reorganized product browsing so oak, hickory and mixed hardwood lead into pizza-cut wood, reduced the partner-logo display, and changed the customer-review grid into horizontal cards. The PDF pair documents these changes; the existing signup and contact sections remain part of the page.

Key Takeaways

Design choices worth testing.

  1. A homepage can explain the offer, provide trust signals and guide a purchase without giving every section equal visual weight.
  2. Buyer education works best when it supports a visible next action, such as selecting a style, exploring products or requesting help.
  3. Before-and-after images demonstrate design decisions. To evaluate business impact, I would compare product/configurator clicks, form completions and purchases over equivalent periods.
