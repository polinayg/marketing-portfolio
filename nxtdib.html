Challenge

Busy buyers. A fast-moving category.

Non-alcoholic and functional beverages change quickly — new formats, new occasions like Dry January, new rules for hemp-derived products. NxtDib needed to be useful to retail and hospitality buyers without turning every post into a sales pitch, on accounts that were still small.

Strategy

Be the buyer's playbook.

  • Answer real buyer questions: what to stock, how to merchandise it, when to reorder, what to ask a brand before a first order.
  • Plan around buying calendars: holiday menus in early November, Dry January stock lists before December shelves are set.
  • Use one recognizable system: teal, warm orange and cream; 4:5 frames; a consistent “Stock what's next.” end slide.
  • Write once, post twice: captions written to work on both Instagram and LinkedIn, plus LinkedIn-native carousels in a quieter editorial style.
My Role

From calendar to caption.

  • Built monthly content calendars and seasonal themes.
  • Wrote every carousel script, caption and CTA in a modern, industry-savvy voice.
  • Produced imagery with ChatGPT image generation, then designed and finished every slide in Canva.
  • Published to Instagram, LinkedIn and Facebook and tracked performance.
Verified: all 114 NxtDib Instagram feed posts in the Sprout export list me as the sender.
Execution

A content system, not one-off posts.

  • Instagram carousels for seasonal stocking: holiday drink menus, Thanksgiving hosting, Dry January, zero-proof merchandising.
  • LinkedIn document carousels in a quieter editorial style: a 10-question first-order checklist, a cooler-door reset, a reorder-point formula.
  • Statics that hold the calendar together between carousels.
  • Volume with consistency: 114 feed posts in the period — 58 carousels, 52 photos and 4 Reels.

Key Takeaways

What I'd carry forward.

  1. In B2B, usefulness is the hook: checklists and formulas beat announcements.
  2. A tight visual system lets AI-assisted production scale without the feed looking generated.
  3. Small audiences deserve honest reporting; the next lever is distribution — LinkedIn and email to buyers — rather than more posts.
