THE CASE STUDY

The challenge

A wholesale beverage marketplace needs to explain fast-moving product categories to busy operators, without turning every post into a platform pitch.

The approach

Build a repeatable content system around retail education, trends, buyer questions, seasonal opportunities, and recognizable brand design.

Execution

Created industry-savvy copy and carousel structures that transform market themes into actionable buyer content, alongside event coverage and campaign graphics.

What the data shows

The account's median post reach was only nine in the period, making high engagement percentages misleading. The portfolio therefore emphasizes the content framework and original creative work rather than claiming audience growth or conversion wins.
