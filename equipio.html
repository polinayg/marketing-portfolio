Challenge

Relevance in a technical community.

Equipio's audience is cinematographers, gaffers, producers and rental houses. They follow accounts that show interesting rigs and real productions. The challenge was to become part of that feed while still steering people to Equipio's value proposition: request a quote for the gear you need instead of hunting vendor by vendor.

Strategy

Earn the follow with craft, then connect to the RFQ.

  • Craft-first curation: standout rig breakdowns and behind-the-scenes clips from working filmmakers, always credited.
  • Original education: carousels that explain the rental and RFQ process in plain language.
  • Channel discipline: Instagram can include credited reposts; LinkedIn gets original content only.
  • One consistent CTA: “Skip the vendor hunt. Upload your RFQ at equipio.com.”
My Role

Curator, writer, publisher.

  • Planned Instagram and LinkedIn calendars (for example, 13 posts a month at three per week for June–August).
  • Found and credited creator content, wrote gear-literate captions and CTAs.
  • Designed original carousels and single-post templates in Canva.
  • Scheduled, published and reviewed performance.
Verified: all 153 Equipio Instagram feed posts in the Sprout export list me as the sender.
Execution

Gear content with a point.

  • Rig breakdowns like recreating the “Severance” camera look, gimbal and stabilizer setups, and precision crane moves.
  • Captions that name the gear — cameras, lenses, rigs — because that's what this audience searches for and saves.
  • RFQ explainers that show how renting through Equipio works.
  • Mix: 103 Reels, 32 carousels and 18 photo posts in the period.

Strong attention, honestly attributed.

  • The highest engagement of any brand in the export, driven mainly by curated Reels: Reels averaged 31.6 engagements per post vs. 8.5 for carousels and 8.9 for photos.
  • Top post: the “Severance” look gear breakdown (Nov 17, 2025) — 282 engagements, 33 shares, 22 saves, 4,047 accounts reached.
  • These are engagement results, not bookings. Reach also varied a lot across the period, which I'd treat as a testing opportunity rather than a growth claim.

Source: Sprout Social Instagram post-performance export, Sept 17, 2025 – Oct 26, 2026. No RFQ, booking or revenue data is included in the export.

Key Takeaways

What I'd carry forward.

  1. In a craft community, specificity is the strategy — name the gear, show the setup.
  2. Curated content builds the audience; original RFQ content has to do the converting. Measure them separately.
  3. Next step: tag RFQ links by post so social can be judged on quote requests.
