THE CASE STUDY

The challenge

Filmmakers care about craft and equipment details; generic marketplace promotion alone may struggle to earn attention.

The approach

Lead with technically interesting cinematography content, rig breakdowns and real production examples, while connecting relevant posts to the rental RFQ value proposition.

Execution

Identified and credited creator content, wrote platform-aligned captions and calls to action, and developed original educational carousel formats about finding and renting equipment.

What the data shows

The Nov 17, 2025 curated Severance-related Reel earned 282 engagements, 33 shares and 22 saves; a Dec 5 rig Reel earned 39 shares. These are engagement outcomes, not booking outcomes. Reach later declined, so the case study frames a future testing opportunity rather than claiming sustained growth.
