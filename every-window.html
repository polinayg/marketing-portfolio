Challenge

A considered purchase, bought online.

Replacement windows are expensive, technical and usually sold in-home. Buyers worry about getting the measurements, styles and quote details wrong. To sell online, Every Window needed content that answers those questions before they become objections — and that looks unmistakably like the brand's own site.

Strategy

Answer the questions a salesperson would.

  • Educate before selling: how quotes differ, single- vs. double-hung, repair vs. replace, condensation, U-factor and SHGC.
  • Make the website the hero: every carousel ends on “Design & price your windows” at everywindow.com.
  • Match the site exactly: the deep-teal, cream and khaki palette, clean sans-serif type and the real Every Window logo.
  • Serve two audiences: homeowners, plus landlords and maintenance teams (rental-turn and PRO Desk content).
My Role

Calendar, copy, design, scheduling.

  • Planned a four-posts-a-week calendar across Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn.
  • Wrote every carousel and caption; designed all slides at 4:5 with AI-generated scene photography finished in the brand system.
  • Exported LinkedIn versions of each carousel as document posts.
  • Loaded the program into Buffer as drafts with channel-specific posting times.
Execution

Nineteen posts, one system.

  • Oct 28 – Nov 27, 2026: 19 posts — 9 carousels and 10 statics — each published to three channels.
  • Repeatable series such as “Measure once” and “Price check” episodes that can run month after month.
  • Carousel anatomy: a photographic cover, cream-background teaching slides with large numerals, and a teal CTA slide.
Results

Launched — results to come.

This program starts publishing on October 28, 2026, after the Sprout Social export used elsewhere on this site, so there is no performance data to report yet. I'm including it for the strategy and the creative system. Results will be added once the month completes.

What I'll measure: saves and shares per carousel, link clicks to the window designer, and which educational topics drive the most visits to pricing.
Key Takeaways

What I'd carry forward.

  1. For considered purchases, content should remove one specific fear per post.
  2. Designing to the website's system builds trust when people click through.
  3. Series formats make a four-a-week cadence sustainable for a small team.
