03 — About

Strategy, creative and follow-through.

I'm the Multi-Brand Marketing Manager at NXT Companies, a vertical-AI venture studio, managing marketing across four B2C and B2B brands.

I connect planning with hands-on execution: social content and publishing, email campaigns in Drip, website builds and redesigns in the CMS, and Google and Meta advertising. My work spans consumer products, wholesale marketplaces, home improvement, and film equipment.

I use GA4, Google Search Console and Sprout Social to understand performance and turn findings into campaign and website recommendations. I adapt the message and visual system to each audience while keeping the work consistent across channels.

AI image generation and writing tools are part of my production workflow, with my editing, design decisions and brand review guiding the final work.
